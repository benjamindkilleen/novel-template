# Synopsis & Outline

## Logline

> One sentence: a protagonist, their goal, the obstacle, the stakes.

## One-paragraph pitch

A short paragraph you could read aloud to describe the book — the hook, the
central conflict, and what makes it different.

## Synopsis

The full story in 1–2 pages, including the ending. Unlike a back-cover blurb,
this spoils everything — it's for you (and agents who request it).

## Outline

Adapt to whatever structure you use (three-act, save-the-cat, none at all).

### Act I — Setup
- [ ] Opening image / status quo
- [ ] Inciting incident
- [ ] First turning point

### Act II — Confrontation
- [ ] Rising complications
- [ ] Midpoint reversal
- [ ] Things fall apart

### Act III — Resolution
- [ ] Climax
- [ ] Resolution / new status quo

## Themes

What the book is *about* underneath the plot. Keep this short and honest —
it's a compass, not a thesis statement.
