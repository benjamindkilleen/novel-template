## Naming conventions

Notes on how names work in your world (patronymics, place-name suffixes,
honorifics) so new names stay consistent.

## Style decisions

- Numbers: spell out under 100? Use figures for years?
- Dialogue: em-dash for interruption, ellipsis for trailing off.
- Made-up words: italicized on first use, roman after.
